# 🌌 SOUL LINK

### An Independent Fantasy VR MMORPG

**A world where adventure becomes your second life.**

SOUL LINK is an independent fantasy MMORPG project currently in development.

Explore a living kingdom, discover ancient magic, build your own life, develop professions, meet intelligent characters and forge your own destiny.

Our ambition is to create an immersive fantasy world designed for PC and virtual reality.

---

## ✨ Our Vision

- 🌍 A persistent and evolving fantasy kingdom.
- ⚔️ Exploration, quests and combat.
- 🔮 Magic, progression and specializations.
- 🏡 Personal homes and virtual life.
- 🛠️ Professions, crafting and economy.
- 🤝 Multiplayer adventures and social experiences.
- 🥽 Immersive VR gameplay.
