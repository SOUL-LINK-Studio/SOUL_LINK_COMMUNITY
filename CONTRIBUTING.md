# 💙 SOUL LINK — Community Guidelines

Welcome to the SOUL LINK community!

SOUL LINK is an independent fantasy VR MMORPG project. Everyone is welcome to follow its development and share constructive feedback.

## How to participate

You can:
- Suggest new ideas through Issues.
- Report bugs and problems.
- Share feedback and encouragement.
- Follow development updates.

## Community rules

1. Be respectful to everyone.
2. Keep discussions constructive.
3. Do not share personal information.
4. Do not post spam, harassment or offensive content.
5. Do not upload confidential files or unauthorized copyrighted material.
6. Respect the project's intellectual property and third-party licenses.

## Important

Community participation does not grant ownership of SOUL LINK or permission to reuse its protected materials.

Thank you for helping SOUL LINK grow!
